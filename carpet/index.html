Training Rooms Scope Note

Two training rooms at 20–25 HC each = ~50 workstations. Estimate ~80–100 sqm of the 2,273.56 sqm full office floor. No separate specification — training rooms get the same primary carpet as the open BPO floor for visual continuity, with optional zoning accent (Tandus Layer CB) if designer requests contrast.

Notes on Volume Pricing (per Charles / April 7)

Charles confirmed that the 38–50% sale discounts visible in the Domus stock file apply only to odd-lot inventory of 5–150 sqm. For a full-floor order of 2,501 sqm, the volume order is priced at SRP with a modest negotiated volume discount (estimate 10–15%, to be confirmed). Prices shown in this dashboard use SRP as the conservative planning baseline. Any volume discount secured through negotiation is upside that further improves the net Dyninno position.
