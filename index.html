Advisory Recommendations

1. Negotiate Break Clause

Secure 3+2 structure with 6-month notice. Reduces maximum exposure by ₱50-90M and provides operational flexibility.

Risk Reduction: ₱50-90M

2. Remove Solidary Liability

Delete group guarantee clause. Each entity should bear only its own obligations. Industry standard for BPO tenants.

Risk Reduction: ₱130.6M

3. Limit Asset Lien

Exclude IT equipment, proprietary systems, and client data infrastructure from lien scope. Critical for operations continuity.

Risk Reduction: ₱15-35M

4. Add Pandemic FM Clause

Include government-mandated closures as force majeure events with proportional rent abatement during restrictions.

Risk Reduction: ₱2.06M/mo

5. Mandatory Cure Period

Replace discretionary 7-day cure with mandatory 30-day written notice before any default remedies can be pursued.

Risk Reduction: Prevents wrongful termination

6. Cap Holdover & Interest

Reduce holdover from 150% to 110-120%, and default interest from 3%/mo to 1%/mo. Align with market norms.

Risk Reduction: ₱4.9M/yr
